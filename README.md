# HubSpot Email Marketing Performance Audit

## Project Overview

This sample project demonstrates how I approach email campaign performance analysis, audience segmentation, email quality assurance, and optimization recommendations.

**Project type:** Sample marketing portfolio project  
**Platform focus:** HubSpot  
**Data:** Fictional sample data created for demonstration purposes

## Objectives

- Evaluate email campaign performance using key metrics.
- Identify opportunities to improve engagement and conversions.
- Review audience segmentation and email quality assurance practices.
- Recommend practical next steps based on campaign results.

## Sample Campaign Performance

The following fictional data illustrates how campaign performance can be reviewed.

| Campaign | Delivered | Opens | Clicks | Conversions |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Product Update | 4,800 | 1,920 | 192 | 24 |
| Educational Newsletter | 7,600 | 2,660 | 304 | 38 |
| Webinar Invitation | 3,800 | 1,520 | 228 | 46 |

### Key Metrics

- **Open rate:** Unique opens divided by delivered emails.
- **Click-through rate (CTR):** Unique clicks divided by delivered emails.
- **Conversion rate:** Conversions divided by delivered emails.

For this sample, open rate and click metrics are illustrative. Actual reporting should use HubSpot's metric definitions and account for privacy-related measurement limitations.

## Initial Observations

- The Educational Newsletter generated the largest number of clicks in this sample.
- The Webinar Invitation had the strongest click-through rate and the most conversions relative to delivered emails.
- The Product Update may benefit from a stronger call to action and more targeted messaging.

These are initial observations from fictional data, not verified client results.

## Recommended Actions

### 1. Audience Segmentation
- Separate audiences by lifecycle stage, customer status, and relevant interests.
- Align campaign messaging with audience needs.
- Suppress ineligible contacts and respect subscription preferences.

### 2. Email Quality Assurance
- Verify personalization tokens, links, and calls to action.
- Review desktop and mobile rendering.
- Test sender details, subject lines, and preview text.
- Check audience exclusions and suppression lists before sending.

### 3. Performance Optimization
- Test subject lines and calls to action where audience size allows.
- Compare performance across relevant audience segments.
- Track clicks and conversions alongside opens.
- Use consistent campaign naming and UTM tracking.

## Reporting Framework

For a real HubSpot campaign audit, I would review:

- Delivery and bounce rates
- Click-through and conversion rates
- Unsubscribe and spam complaint rates
- Performance by segment and campaign type
- Landing-page outcomes and lead quality
